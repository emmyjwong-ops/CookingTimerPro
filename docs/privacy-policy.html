Privacy Policy — Cooking Timer Pro

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Cooking Timer Pro ("the app") is developed by EmmyJW. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Cooking Timer Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data (timers, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device only.

In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time premium upgrade processed through Google Play. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play and are subject to Google's privacy policy. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to display timer countdowns and alerts.
  • Alarms & reminders — to trigger timer alarms at exact times, even when the screen is off.
  • Vibration — to vibrate the device when a timer completes.

None of these permissions are used to collect data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL.
